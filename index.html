01 — The Seasonal Edit

Every season has its own
palette & rhythm.

Spring blooms, summer glows, autumn grounds, winter rests. Vitto translates each season's mood into a plan worth living by — colors, rituals and intentions, curated twice a year.

Season 01

Spring — March to May

Fresh, light and full of promise. Soft petals, longer mornings and the gentle art of beginning again — a season for clearing space and growing.

  • Reset your space & wardrobe
  • Embrace light, airy layering
  • Plan the season of new starts

Season 02

Summer — June to August

Golden, generous, alive. The longest light of the year invites slow mornings, open water and warm nights — plan for presence over busyness.

  • Chase golden hour, slowly
  • Plan escapes & long swims
  • Savor meals outside
GlowSwimTravelFeast

Season 03

Autumn — September to November

Warm, grounded, richly textured. Amber light, harvested fields and the slow return indoors — a season to gather, reflect and layer with intention.

  • Welcome the cozy rituals
  • Layer textures & warm tones
  • Gather friends around the table
HarvestReflectLayerGather

Season 04

Winter — December to February

Quiet, still, softly bright. The year folds inward for rest and warmth — candlelight, deep sleep and the quiet planning of what comes next.

  • Rest deeply, on purpose
  • Warm gatherings, soft glow
  • Set intentions for the new year
